DM summary

DOP = POS GPA + pickup GPA − frontline TM, office, disposal, rental, operating, WH & logistics, capex and marketing costs. 100% commission target and the commission levels come from the " Target %" tab; Commission now = the highest level the Target tab's projected DOP reaches (0% below the 50% level). Projected DOP, achievement and difference come from the Target tab (excludes outlets; it projects with its own day count).

AM summary

Projected DOP = DOP × days in month ÷ days of POS sales, as in the source sheet. DOP ach. % follows the Target tab: projected DOP ÷ target, or target ÷ projected DOP when the target is negative.

All shops

How the cost is countedEach cost is basic + non-basic pay, plus: 15% overhead for DM, AM, UM, SS, ASS and SA · 7% for PT (plus $1,000 per 468PT) · $1,000 transport allowance for SA–UM at the remote shops. OT is $60/hour and outsource is $65/hour.

Where the TM cost goes

All shops, current TM cost by group, HK$. DM, AM and UM are combined in the source sheet; UM will move to the UM + SS line once the UM split is added.

TM cost by shop

Costs include overhead as above. Projected TM cost = full-month cost per shop based on the roster.

Daily TM cost

Overall frontline TM cost per day (FT, PT, outsource and overheads), summed from each shop's daily "TM Cost (Frontline)" in the DOP bot sheet. "vs yesterday" compares with the day before (an increase is red). "Of which PT + OS" is that day's PT (incl. 7%) and outsource cost from the TM cost sheet, shown for all shops only. In the chart the darker part of each bar is PT + OS.

Staff cost distribution by shop

Each group's share of the shop's current TM cost. The four shares add up to 100%.
